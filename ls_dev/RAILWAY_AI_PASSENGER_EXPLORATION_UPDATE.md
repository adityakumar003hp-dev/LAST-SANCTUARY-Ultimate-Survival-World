# Railway AI Passenger Exploration Update

## Railway operation
- All six lines (F1, F2, F3, B1, B2 and B3) are AI-operated.
- F1 and B1 are no longer player-driven trains.
- AI services move along their assigned direction, stop at generated stations, open their passenger doors, dwell, close doors and depart.
- Passenger announcements use the browser speech engine when supported, with existing station chimes and brake audio retained. Announcements cover an approaching station, arrival, and departure.

## Passenger experience
- Every train has three connected cars: one powered lead car and two passenger coaches.
- Passenger seats are interactable. Board at a station while doors are open, walk close to a seat and press E to sit; press E again to stand.
- The passenger movement area spans the full three-car consist. Open central gangways and short bridge floors connect the cars.
- While aboard, drag on the game view to orbit the camera horizontally through 360 degrees and adjust the vertical angle. This supports mouse and touch pointer input.
- The train HUD receives the current service, next-station and announcement information while aboard.

## Controls
- Approach a stopped train while its doors are open and press E to board.
- Use the existing movement controls to walk between the three cars.
- Press E near a passenger seat to sit; press E again to stand.
- Drag the game view to look around; press E away from a seat to exit the train.

## Compatibility note
Speech announcements depend on browser speech-synthesis support and may use the device's installed voice. If speech is unavailable or blocked by browser policy, train movement and the existing audio cues continue normally.
