# LAST SANCTUARY — THE LAST DRIVE (Apartment Compound Storyline)

## Core premise
The player and three friends live in a modern four-floor apartment inside a walled residential compound in Oakridge. The compound is a deliberate safe starting zone: clean paved grounds, no wild vegetation or debris inside the perimeter, a secure double gate, a private garage and one parked sedan. The rooftop is a practical emergency lookout and solar-power deck, not a swimming pool.

## Day 1 — A Normal Morning
1. **Home Sweet Home:** wake in the apartment, explore the rooms and the staircase. The stair flights now have continuous handrails and protective balusters.
2. **The Garage Key:** inspect the private garage and the parked blue sedan. The car is initially locked; its key is on the kitchen counter beside a note from Marcus.
3. **The Compound:** walk the clean driveway and inspect the perimeter wall, gate motor, exterior lights and garage shutter.
4. **Corner Roast:** leave through the compound gate and meet Marcus, Chloe and Dr. Maya at the café.
5. **A Strange Signal:** Chloe reports electromagnetic interference from the Pine Ridge repeater. The news reports an unusual object approaching Earth.

## Day 2 — The First Warning
6. **Return Behind the Walls:** regroup at the apartment as emergency alerts begin. Close the compound gate and check the garage battery, fuel reserve and emergency supplies.
7. **The Rooftop Watch:** climb the railed staircase to the open rooftop emergency deck. Use the solar array and radio mast to triangulate the incoming impact corridor.
8. **The First Impact:** a distant meteorite strike triggers power failures, fires and the first infected sightings beyond the compound. The walls delay the first wave, but the gate motor is damaged.
9. **Manual Override:** descend safely, restore the gate mechanism and move the sedan out of the garage. The car becomes the player's first evacuation vehicle.
10. **Get Maya to the Clinic:** drive through blocked streets to reach the clinic and recover medical supplies. The friends discover that the outbreak is spreading faster than official broadcasts admit.

## Day 3 — Beyond Oakridge
11. **The Repeater Run:** take Chloe to Pine Ridge to restore a long-range emergency signal.
12. **The Evacuation List:** rescue stranded residents and bring them to the apartment compound. The compound changes from a private home into a defended refuge.
13. **A Sanctuary, Not a Prison:** repair the garage, reinforce the gate, install lights and establish a supply route. The player must decide when to defend the compound and when to risk a supply run.
14. **The Last Transmission:** a broken broadcast reveals that the meteorite carried an adaptive organism. The friends set out to find the source and locate a permanent sanctuary.

## Gameplay and mission rules
- Keep the apartment compound bounded by its continuous perimeter walls and double gate.
- No procedural trees, rocks, scrap, berry bushes, or residential houses may spawn inside the compound boundary.
- Keep the compound grounds readable and navigable; authored furniture, landscaping, driveway, garage and vehicle are allowed.
- The staircase must have visible handrails and balusters on both sides of every flight, with collision/navigation clearance preserved.
- Remove the rooftop swimming pool completely. Replace it with a walkable emergency lookout deck, pergola/seating and solar panels.
- The private garage and sedan are authored scene objects. Do not place the sedan across the garage opening or in the player's spawn/collision path.
- Preserve existing apartment floors, rooms, doors, story characters, controls, save data, railway and unrelated survival systems.
