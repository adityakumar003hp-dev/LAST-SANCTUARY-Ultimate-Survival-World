What is LAST SANCTUARY?

LAST SANCTUARY: ULTIMATE SURVIVAL WORLD is a 3D open-world survival game that runs in a modern web browser. Explore a large environment, encounter infected threats and wildlife, collect resources, interact with survivors, and work toward establishing a secure place to live.

The experience combines exploration, survival management, action, and world-building. Players can move through different environments, discover locations, use vehicles, and take part in a story-led survival journey.

Explore, survive and build

Open-world exploration

Travel across varied environments and discover different areas, landmarks, resources and points of interest.

Zombie survival

Stay alert to infected threats, manage your condition, prepare supplies and make careful decisions while exploring.

Wildlife and ecosystems

Encounter wildlife as part of the living environment and use the world’s resources to support survival.

Sanctuary building

Gather materials, craft useful equipment, construct structures and strengthen defensive areas.

Vehicles and railway travel

Use available vehicles and the railway network to move through the game world and reach new destinations.

Survivors and story

Meet other characters, take on objectives and progress through a story-driven survival experience.

Game modes and progression

Choose from the Story Campaign, Survival RPG, or Creative Builder mode when starting a new world. The story campaign follows an origin narrative, Survival RPG focuses on gathering resources and managing survival needs, and Creative Builder supports peaceful construction.

37 biomes and a living world

The world is built around 37 biome types, including forests, grasslands, settlements, cities, wetlands, deserts, frozen regions, caves, volcanic areas and dinosaur habitats. Wildlife species are grouped across mammals, birds, dinosaurs, aquatic animals, reptiles, amphibians, insects and rare encounters.

Exploration is connected to resource gathering, discovering locations, scanning wildlife and expanding your field knowledge through the in-game codex.

AI-operated railway travel

The railway network uses AI-operated train services. Players can use the station and ticket interfaces, board passenger trains, travel in the connected carriages and explore the train interior. The locomotive remains under autonomous AI control; the cab includes an observer seat rather than player-operated train driving.

Browser access and saved progress

LAST SANCTUARY runs as a web game built with React and Three.js. The project includes Progressive Web App support, local save slots, and JSON save export and import options. Actual performance depends on the device, browser and available memory.

How to play

Open the game homepage and choose a game mode from the main menu. Use the in-game controls and Survivor Guide to learn movement, interaction, inventory, crafting and survival features. The experience is designed for modern desktop and mobile web browsers; actual performance depends on the device and browser.
