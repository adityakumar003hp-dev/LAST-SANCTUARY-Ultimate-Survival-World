# LAST SANCTUARY: REBIRTH — 12-DAY CAMPAIGN

The active campaign is now 12 days × 8 missions = 96 missions. The player remains in the existing game world and uses its existing locations, survival systems, military base, sanctuary, vehicles and AI-operated railway.

## Four friends
- **Chloe Miller — Explorer:** scouting, route discovery and communications context.
- **Dr. Maya Lin — Doctor:** medical supplies, triage and survivor care.
- **Marcus Cole — Saviour:** rescue leadership and civilian protection.
- **Rohan Mehta — Armoury & Tactical:** equipment inventory, protective readiness and tactical planning.

## Cinematic milestones
The Quiet Before; A Sky Turning Strange; The Impact; First Night; The Last Departure; The Gate Holds; The Lifeline; A Place to Call Home; The Hidden Destination; The Truth Beneath; The Road Is Open; The Five Together; Final Morning; The Truth Revealed; The Last Stand; and the selected ending. Milestone scenes use a location-relative camera orbit and the existing letterbox/title overlay.

## Branching endings
- **A New Dawn:** remain at the sanctuary and rebuild locally.
- **United Survivors:** connect settlements into a regional support network.
- **Unknown Horizon:** follow the distant signal beyond the known region.

## Continuity rules
- Existing locations and systems are reused; this campaign does not create replacement buildings or fictional station machinery.
- Railway services remain AI-operated. The player boards as a passenger and does not drive locomotives.
- Station missions use existing platforms, service information and passenger boarding. No station generator or station-power restoration mission exists. The existing military-base generator interaction is reserved for Day 4, after the railway journey.
- Existing save fields are retained. Old saves remain loadable; the new campaign uses the expanded mission range and preserves completed mission numbers and story flags.
- Mission 96 unlocks permanent free exploration without resetting the world.

## Runtime integration
Campaign objectives advance through arrival at the authored mission location and player interactions near that location. The ending decision is explicitly selected in the Chronicle during Mission 94 and stored in the story checkpoint. Cinematic milestones play a short orbit around the current player location and show the scene title/beat.

The campaign has been source-integrated but still requires a full browser/mobile playthrough to validate every objective against every authored world interaction.
