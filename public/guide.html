1. Game Modes & Story Campaign

  • Story Campaign ("The Beginning of the End"): Follow a 13-chapter origin narrative beginning before the outbreak, meeting companions Marcus, Chloe, and Maya inside the sanctuary district before the meteor impact and zombie siege.
  • Open-World Survival Sandbox: Explore 37 interconnected procedural biomes, harvest timber, ore, and electronics, manage hunger, thirst, oxygen, and night Blood Moon hordes.
  • Creative Builder Mode: Construct multi-tier fortifications, farm plots, watchtowers, and defensive walls freely without starvation or lethal pressure.

3. 37 Biomes & Evolving Ecosystem

Travel across verdant meadowlands, conifer wildwoods, abandoned metropolis ruins, volcanic ashlands, frozen glacial ridges, bioluminescent caverns, savannahs, and prehistoric dinosaur valleys. Wildlife species include predators, grazers, birds and aquatic animals, with behaviors such as hunger, thirst, herding, predator-prey hunting, reproduction and taming.

4. Central Mega-Station & Sanctuary Base Terminal

  • Railway Network: Visit the multi-level Mega Railway Station to book tickets, track six AI-operated regional train lines, board moving carriages, and manage live dispatch schedules with T. Train movement remains under autonomous AI control.
  • Sanctuary Estate Terminal (O): Monitor rooftop solar production and backup diesel generators, UV clean water filtration, cold-storage rations, perimeter CCTV security feeds, survivor NPC assignments, and research blueprints.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

What is LAST SANCTUARY: ULTIMATE SURVIVAL WORLD?

It is a browser-based 3D open-world zombie survival RPG built with Three.js and React, featuring 37 procedural biomes, a diverse wildlife ecosystem, a multi-level railway mega-station, base management, and an episodic story campaign.

Can I play offline or back up my saves?

The game includes installable Progressive Web App support with cached assets. Save slots are stored locally in the browser and can be exported or restored as JSON backup files.

Can I drive the trains myself?

No. Railway services are AI-operated. You can board the trains, walk through connected carriages and sit in the locomotive cab's observer seat while the AI controls the train.

How does Photo Mode work?

Press P or tap the camera button in the HUD to toggle HUD-free framing and capture high-resolution PNG screenshots directly from the 3D WebGL viewport.
